# Former Dawgs Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 3, 2026

Former Dawgs provides baseball statistics, stories, and video links about former Mississippi State players.

## Information We Collect

Former Dawgs does not require account creation and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, or payment information.

The app may:

- store local preferences on your device, such as selected player, favorites, and appearance settings
- retrieve public baseball statistics, stories, and video links from third-party sources over the internet

## How Information Is Used

Any locally stored preferences are used only to improve your in-app experience, such as remembering your selected player or favorites.

## Data Sharing

Former Dawgs does not sell or share your personal information.

The app links to third-party websites and services for news, highlights, and player information. Those third parties may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

## Third-Party Services

Former Dawgs may display or link to content from sources such as:

- MLB
- Google News
- YouTube

Use of those services is subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

## Data Retention

App preferences stored locally on your device remain there until you remove the app or clear the app’s data by deleting it.

## Children’s Privacy

Former Dawgs is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your Choices

You can stop all data use by uninstalling the app from your device.
