# Former Dawgs Support

If you need help with Former Dawgs, use the information below.

## Support Contact

Email: `fans_wig.0j@icloud.com`

## What to Include

If you report a problem, include:

- your device model
- your iOS or iPadOS version
- the app version
- a short description of the issue
- screenshots if relevant

## Common Help Topics

- App not loading stats or stories
- Player data looks outdated
- Favorite players are not saving
- App layout issues on iPad
- Questions about featured players or content sources

## Response Time

Support responses are typically provided as availability permits.
